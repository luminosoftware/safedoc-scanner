This Privacy Policy explains how SafeDoc Scanner ("SafeDoc Scanner", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our application. The App is developed by Luminotechnology Pvt. Ltd., Srijana Chowk, Pokhara, Nepal.

SafeDoc Scanner is a document scanning and document processing application that allows you to scan documents, extract text, crop and enhance images, create PDFs, convert PDFs to images, and create or edit spreadsheet files.

Our core document-processing features are designed to operate locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores the documents you scan, import, create, or edit.

You can read this policy at any time from within the App (Profile → Privacy Policy) or at this web address. Separate consent may be requested by advertising or other third-party components where applicable.

1. What SafeDoc Scanner Does

SafeDoc Scanner provides tools for working with documents directly on your device, including:

  • Scanning documents using your device camera.
  • Detecting and cropping document pages.
  • Correcting document perspective and orientation.
  • Applying filters and image enhancements.
  • Extracting text from document images using OCR.
  • Converting images into PDF documents.
  • Converting PDF pages into images.
  • Importing documents from your device.
  • Creating and editing spreadsheet files, including columns and text.
  • Saving and exporting documents.
  • Sharing documents through your device's system Share Sheet.

These operations are designed to be performed locally on your device.

2. No SafeDoc Document Server

SafeDoc Scanner does not upload your documents to our servers. The App does not provide a cloud storage service and does not send your document contents to a Luminotechnology Pvt. Ltd. server.

The following content is processed locally on your device:

  • Scanned images and imported images.
  • Imported PDF, Excel, and text files.
  • OCR-extracted text.
  • Cropped and enhanced document images.
  • Image pages produced by converting a PDF to images.
  • Generated PDF and Excel files.

Device backups and copies you choose to export or share are separate and are described in this policy. See Section 16 for the Google services used by the App.

3. How Your Documents Are Stored

SafeDoc Scanner uses storage provided by your device. The App stores its working document library in the application's private storage area.

Scanned Pages and Imported Files

Stored in the App's private scanned_pages storage, using application-generated identifiers rather than relying solely on the original filename.

PDF Documents

PDF files created from images are saved into the same private storage. A short-lived temporary working copy may be created while a PDF is being built or converted, and is removed once the operation completes.

Excel Documents

Excel files created through the spreadsheet feature are saved into private storage. A temporary working copy is created while saving and is removed once the save completes.

Converted PDF Pages

When you save the pages of a PDF you converted to images, the App copies those images into private storage so they remain in your library.

Document Library Information

The App maintains a local index containing information needed to organize your library, such as the document title, creation date, and file location within the App's storage. This index is stored locally on your device.

The operating system may include app-private files in device backups when backup is enabled. Android may back up app files through Google device backup, and iOS may include app data in iCloud or computer backups. These backups are managed by your device platform, not by SafeDoc Scanner.

4. Temporary Files

During scanning and document processing, the App may temporarily store camera captures, cropped images, or other intermediate files in the device's temporary storage. When the relevant operation is completed or cancelled, the App is designed to remove temporary files that are no longer required.

5. Copies Saved Outside the App

Some features make documents accessible outside the App. These copies are created only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Scanned and Imported Images

When you save a scanned document, or the pages of a PDF you converted to images, the App may create a copy in your device's gallery, in an album named:

SafeDoc Scanner

Images are saved to the gallery rather than the Downloads folder.

Imported PDF, Excel, and Text Files

On Android, the App attempts to create a copy in the device Downloads folder. This is best-effort and may fail, for example if the required legacy storage permission is denied on Android 9 or earlier. On iOS and other platforms, this Downloads export is not provided. Spreadsheet files can be imported as .xlsx or .csv; legacy .xls workbooks are not supported.

Files Created Inside the App

Files the App creates or converts for you (such as edited Excel workbooks and PDFs produced from images) are stored in the private library and also copied to Android Downloads on the same best-effort basis. If that copy cannot be written, the App keeps the file in its library and tells you.

Saving a spreadsheet always produces a new .xlsx workbook and never modifies the file you opened. Saving a .csv file therefore converts it to .xlsx. Use Share if you want to send a file to another app or service.

6. Public or User-Accessible Copies

Files stored in the device's gallery or Downloads folder may be accessible to other applications that have permission to access those locations. Documents stored outside the App's private storage may therefore have different privacy protections from documents inside it.

You are responsible for managing copies you choose to store in publicly accessible locations.

7. Sharing Documents

SafeDoc Scanner uses your device's system Share Sheet when you choose to share a document. For example, you may share a PDF or Excel file with:

  • Google Drive.
  • Email applications.
  • Messaging applications.
  • Cloud-storage applications.
  • Other compatible applications.

The App does not decide where a shared document is sent. Once you share a document, the receiving application handles it under its own functionality and privacy policy.

8. Deleting Documents

When you delete a document in SafeDoc Scanner, the App removes it from its private application storage and removes its entry from the local document index.

Copies previously created outside the App may remain, for example:

  • A gallery copy in the SafeDoc Scanner album.
  • A file saved in the Downloads folder.
  • A copy in a device backup, if backup is enabled.

Deleting a document from the App does not necessarily delete these external copies. You can manage them through your device, your backup provider, or the app where you shared the file.

9. OCR and Text Extraction

OCR is performed locally on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device Text Recognition (see Section 16). The App does not upload your document images or extracted text to our servers.

OCR results may contain errors depending on image quality, layout, language, font, lighting, handwriting, and other factors. Please verify extracted text before relying on it for important purposes.

10. Image Processing and Document Enhancement

The App provides local image-processing features including, where supported:

  • Document cropping and edge detection.
  • Perspective correction.
  • Rotation.
  • Filters and image enhancement.
  • Brightness and contrast adjustments.

These operations are performed on your device using the bundled OpenCV component (see Section 16). Your document images are not uploaded to our servers for these operations.

11. PDF and Excel Processing

Image-to-PDF conversion, PDF-to-image conversion, spreadsheet creation, spreadsheet editing, and saving are performed locally on your device. The resulting files are not uploaded to Luminotechnology Pvt. Ltd. servers.

12. Camera and Device Permissions

Camera

Required for capturing documents. Camera images are processed locally. You can grant or revoke access at any time in your device settings. If denied, camera-based scanning will not work.

Photos and Files

On Android, image import uses the system photo picker, which lets you choose specific images without granting broad photo-library access. On iOS, image import and saving scans to Photos may use the system photo library prompts. Gallery saving is best-effort and may require permission.

Storage (Android 9 and earlier)

The App may request legacy storage permission only when it attempts to export files to Downloads. It is not needed on Android 10 and later.

Internet

Supports Google AdMob advertising, Google User Messaging Platform consent, and Google Play services used for document scanning. The App's own document library and file processing do not upload document contents to a SafeDoc Scanner server.

Advertising ID

The advertising SDK may add advertising-related permissions (such as the Android advertising ID and network state) through manifest merging. See Section 13.

You can review and change permissions in your device settings.

13. Advertising and Google AdMob

SafeDoc Scanner uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

While the App does not upload your documents to our servers, the AdMob SDK may collect or process certain information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services. Depending on your device, settings, location, applicable law, and Google's services, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID).
  • Device-related information.
  • IP address.
  • Approximate location derived from network information.
  • Advertising interaction and ad performance information.

AdMob does not receive your scanned documents simply because you use the App's document features.

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may show a consent form the first time you open the App. Your choice is recorded and applied to the advertising served in the App. Where required, you can change your choice later in the App under Profile → Privacy & Ad Choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

14. Information We Do Not Collect

SafeDoc Scanner is designed not to collect or upload the contents of your documents. We do not intentionally collect or upload:

  • Your scanned document images.
  • Your imported PDF, Excel, or text-file contents.
  • OCR text extracted from your documents.
  • PDFs and Excel files created by the App.
  • Document images created or edited by the App.

These files are processed and stored locally on your device.

The App does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service, and we do not create user accounts. However, the Google components integrated in the App (AdMob, UMP, and Google Play services / ML Kit) may collect limited technical information, such as device information, app identifiers, and performance or diagnostic data, under Google's own privacy practices (see Section 16).

15. Internet Connectivity

The core document-processing features are designed to operate without uploading documents to an online service. The App may use an internet connection for Google AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform, and Google Play services components. An internet connection may therefore be used even though your documents are processed locally.

16. Third-Party Software and Services

On-device components

  • Google ML Kit and Google Play services – document scanning and on-device text recognition (OCR).
  • OpenCV – open-source computer-vision library used for cropping, perspective correction, and image enhancement.
  • A local document-detection library – used to find document edges in a photo.

Internet services (Google)

  • Google AdMob – display advertising (see Section 13).
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – requests and manages advertising consent.

These services operate independently and may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them. This policy does not make claims about data handled independently by Google.

17. Data Security

We take reasonable measures within the App's design to keep your documents in local application storage and avoid unnecessary transmission of document contents. No electronic storage system is completely secure, and the security of your files also depends on your device and operating system.

We recommend using:

  • A device passcode or biometric lock.
  • Current operating-system security updates.
  • Trusted applications.
  • Appropriate permissions and device security settings.

Please also consider the sensitivity of any document before sharing it with another application.

18. Children's Privacy

SafeDoc Scanner is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not operate a cloud database of scanned documents. Advertising SDKs may process device and advertising data as described in Sections 13 and 16.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request or another communication channel, please contact us and we will delete it.

19. Your Responsibilities

Because the App processes and stores documents locally, you control the documents on your device. You are responsible for:

  • Reviewing documents before sharing them.
  • Managing files stored in your gallery or Downloads folder.
  • Deleting documents you no longer need.
  • Protecting your device from unauthorized access.
  • Understanding how third-party applications handle files you choose to share with them.

20. Your Choices and Rights

  • Documents: you can view, share, rename, and delete your documents inside the App at any time. Because we do not hold your documents on a server, there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on your behalf.
  • Advertising consent: where available, you can change your consent under Profile → Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Permissions: you can revoke camera, photo, and storage permissions in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling: uninstalling the App removes its private library from your device. Copies in your gallery, Downloads folder, backups, or other apps remain until you delete them.
  • Privacy requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as access, correction, or deletion. We do not hold your document contents. For data handled by Google AdMob, please use Google's own controls and privacy tools. You can also contact us using the details in Section 23.

21. Data Retention

Your documents are kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We do not retain copies of your documents. Information handled by Google services is retained according to Google's policies.

24. Privacy at a Glance

  • Documents are stored locally by default. We do not upload your scanned documents, imported documents, OCR text, PDFs, images, or Excel files to our own servers. If you export or share a file, it may be copied elsewhere or sent to the app you select.
  • Document processing happens locally. Scanning, OCR, cropping, enhancement, PDF conversion, and spreadsheet processing run on your device.
  • Your files may have copies outside the App. Scans and imported images may be saved to the gallery, files may be copied to Android Downloads, and files you share may be sent to another app.
  • Deleting inside the App does not necessarily delete external copies.
  • Sharing is under your control. You choose which application receives a file.
  • Advertising is separate from your documents. The App uses Google AdMob, which may collect advertising-related device or identifier information under Google's policies.
  • We do not sell your documents.
